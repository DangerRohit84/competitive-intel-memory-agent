# Demo script — 120 seconds

Total: 2:00. Practice once with the UI open at http://localhost:8501.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem (20s)

> "Every rep has lost a deal to a competitor they barely knew.
> Pricing quotes live in call notes, feature gaps in Slack, win stories in
> someone's head. By RFP time, the context is gone and the battlecard is
> generic. We fixed that with a memory-backed agent — every intel note is
> retained in Hindsight and recalled exactly when the next RFP lands."

[Show UI title + empty right pane.]

## 0:20–0:50 — Cold generic (30s)

> "Here is the before. I paste a 400-seat logistics RFP against Acme —
> needs SSO, audit logs, India hosting, per-seat pricing — and generate
> with NO memory."

[Click Generate battlecard / show Cold column side-by-side.]

> "See? Secure, scalable platform, competitive pricing, trusted everywhere.
> True, and useless. No numbers, no traps, no proof."

## 0:50–1:30 — Retain 2 notes (40s)

> "Now we teach it. Two real notes from last month."

[Type + Retain new intel x2:]

1. Type pricing — "Acme quoted $48K/yr for 200 seats to Northwind, 12% under us."
   Click Retain. Toast: Retained.
2. Type win — "Won vs Acme at Globex $96K — won on 365-day audit + Mumbai hosting."
   Click Retain.

> "Each retain stores text plus context 'competitive intel pricing/win',
> stable document ID, and tag competitor:Acme. That is the Hindsight write path."

## 1:30–2:00 — Recalled battlecard + impact (30s)

> "Same RFP, now with memory."

[Click Generate battlecard. Show With-memory column.]

> "Pricing intel with the $48K number, trap on 30-day audit logs,
> proof from the Globex win — with Memory 1, 2 citations and a recall trace.
> Win-rate dashboard updates from the seed data. Cold was generic;
> recalled wins deals. That is agent memory."

[End on side-by-side + links in README.]
